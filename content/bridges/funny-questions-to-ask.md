# Funny Questions to Ask: 100 That Make the Answer Funny

There are two kinds of funny question and only one of them works.

The first has the joke inside it. *If you were a hot dog, would you eat yourself?* It gets a laugh — at the question. Then somebody has to answer, and there is nothing to say, because the funny part already happened and it was not theirs. Thirty seconds later the room is quieter than before you asked.

The second kind is not funny at all on its own. *What is the worst thing currently in your fridge?* Nobody laughs when you ask it. They laugh at the answer, and then at the next person's answer, and then somebody says "hang on, why do you have that", and you are ten minutes into the best part of the evening.

**A funny question is one that makes the other person funny.** All 100 below are the second kind.

## Why the Zany Ones Fall Flat

`performing-cleverness` is the improv name for the failure: reaching for something impressive rather than something true. It is the most common way a scene dies and the most common way a question does.

An invented, absurd question puts the other person in an impossible position. They can play along with a bit they did not write, or they can be the person who takes it too seriously. Neither is comfortable, and neither is funny.

`obvious-choice` is the corrective. Your first thought is connected to the situation; your second is connected to your ego. The plain, specific, slightly embarrassing question is nearly always the first thought, and it works because the answer is real — and real is funnier than invented, because nobody can predict it.

The mundane part is not a compromise. It is the mechanism. Comedy lives in detail, and only true things have detail.

## Questions About Everyday Indignity

- What is the worst thing currently in your fridge?
- What is the most embarrassing thing you have done sober?
- What is the last thing you did that you would not want filmed?
- What is the most inconvenient place you have fallen asleep?
- What is something you have broken and never confessed to?
- What is the worst haircut you have had, and how long did it last?
- What is the most awkward way you have injured yourself?
- What is a lie you told that spiralled?
- What is the worst thing you have said to the wrong person?
- What have you done to avoid a phone call?
- What is the longest you have gone without doing something obvious, like a haircut or a dentist?
- What is the pettiest revenge you have taken?
- What is the worst outfit you have willingly worn?
- What is the most embarrassing thing in your house right now?
- What is a thing you have pretended to know about?
- What have you Googled that you would not say out loud?
- What is the worst thing you have found in your own pocket?
- What have you done that you blamed on someone else?
- What is the most unnecessary thing you have got upset about this year?
- What is a photo of you that should not exist?

- What is a piece of clothing you kept far too long?
- What is the worst haircut you have asked for on purpose?
- What is a hobby you took up and abandoned inside a month?
- What is the most money you have wasted on something you used once?
- What is the worst film you have paid to see twice?
- What is a phase you had that your family still bring up?
- What is the worst advice you have confidently given?
- What is a decision you defended for years and now cannot explain?
- What is something you were absolutely certain about and wrong about?
- What is the worst place you have chosen for a first date?
- What is a subscription you are still paying for?
- What is the most useless thing you own that you refuse to throw out?
- What is a skill you claimed on a CV that you did not have?
- What is a food combination you defend that nobody else will?
- What is the worst nickname you have had, and did you earn it?
- What is a tattoo, haircut or piercing you nearly got?
- What did you spend your first proper wages on?
- What is a job you quit in a way you regret?
- What is the worst thing you have said in an interview?

## Questions About Family and Growing Up

Reliable, because everybody has an answer and nobody has to perform.

- What is a rule your family had that other families would find baffling?
- What did you get told off for most as a child?
- What is the strangest thing your family considers normal?
- What did you spend your pocket money on?
- What was the worst haircut your parents chose for you?
- What is a family story that gets retold and is definitely exaggerated?
- What is something you believed for far too long because a relative told you?
- What was the worst holiday your family took?
- What is your family's most useless heirloom?
- What did you steal from your parents?
- What is the pettiest thing your siblings still hold against you?
- What was the worst meal you were made to finish?
- What is the most confident you have ever been while completely wrong?
- What is the strangest thing your grandparents said with total confidence?
- What was the thing you were unaccountably obsessed with at nine?
- What is a birthday present that went wrong?
- What did your family argue about in the car?
- What is something you did as a kid that would horrify you now?
- What is a nickname a relative gave you that stuck?
- What is the most embarrassing thing a parent has said to your friends?

- What is a food everyone likes that you find genuinely upsetting?
- What is a film everyone loves that you cannot sit through?
- What brand or product would you defend in an argument?
- What is a small thing you are unreasonably competitive about?
- What is a chore you enjoy that you should not?
- What is the pettiest reason you have decided you dislike someone?
- What is a rule you follow that nobody asked you to?
- What is something popular that you have decided is a conspiracy against you?

- What is a compliment you would find insulting?
- What is a thing you pretend to like?
- What is a rule of grammar you enforce and cannot justify?
- What is something you would ban if given one ban?

## Questions for a Group That Will Run With It

These open a pattern rather than closing with a punchline. `game-of-the-scene` is the improv term — find the thing that is fun and do it again, bigger.

- What is the worst job anyone here has had, and can anyone beat it?
- What is the most useless skill around this table?
- What is something we have all done that nobody admits to?
- What is the worst thing anyone here has eaten on purpose?
- What is everyone's most embarrassing musical phase?
- What is a small thing that would ruin anyone's day?
- What is the worst present anyone here has received?
- What is everyone's least defensible opinion?
- Who here has the strangest family tradition?
- What is the worst haircut in this room, historically?
- What is a thing everyone here pretends to understand?
- What is the most inconvenient place anyone here has got lost?
- What is the pettiest argument anyone here has won?
- What is everyone's worst holiday story?
- What is a thing we would all be terrible at?
- What is the most pointless rule anyone here has had to follow?
- What is the worst nickname in this room?
- What is a rule everyone here breaks?
- What is something everyone here owns and cannot explain?
- What is the most unnecessary thing anyone bought this year?

## Funny Questions That Do Not Work

Each of these is on every list and each fails the same way: the joke is in the question, so the answer has nowhere to go.

- **"If you were a kitchen appliance, which would you be?"** The whole joke is the premise. The answer is a noun.
- **"Would you rather fight one horse-sized duck or a hundred duck-sized horses?"** Genuinely funny once, in 2010. Everyone has now heard it and has a prepared answer.
- **"What's your spirit animal?"** Asks for a self-classification. Categories close conversations; specifics open them.
- **"If you could have dinner with anyone, dead or alive..."** Produces a name and then silence. Every possible answer is a dead end.
- **"How many chickens could you fight?"** A bit, not a question. The other person has to either join the bit or decline it.
- **"What would you do in a zombie apocalypse?"** Invites a rehearsed monologue about a situation nobody has been in.
- **"Tell us a funny story."** Puts someone on the spot to perform, which is the fastest way to make them not funny.
- **"What's the funniest thing that's ever happened to you?"** Same, with the added cruelty of demanding their best material first.
- **"If you were a hot dog, would you eat yourself?"** The joke is complete on delivery.
- **Anything beginning "if you could".** Hypotheticals have no detail in them, and detail is where comedy is.

## How to Get the Laugh

The question is a third of it. The rest is what happens after.

**Go second.** Answer your own question first only if nobody will start — otherwise let somebody else set the level, then match it. Whoever goes first decides how honest the round will be.

**Ask the follow-up, not the next question.** Somebody says the worst thing in their fridge is a jar of something from 2019. The laugh is not there yet. "Why is it still there?" is where it is.

**Heighten rather than move on.** `heightening` is taking what was funny and doing more of it. If one person's answer lands, ask the same question of the person next to them rather than reaching for a new question. A run of four answers to the same question is funnier than four different questions, every time.

**Do not explain why it was funny.** The most common way a group kills its own laugh.

**Be prepared to be the answer.** A question you would not answer yourself is not a funny question, it is a trap, and everybody can tell.

## Practise the Building Part

**Accepting and adding.** `yes-and-chain` requires each person to accept the previous statement and build on it, with no reframes and no "yes, but". It is the mechanism behind a run of answers getting funnier rather than a sequence of unrelated jokes.
