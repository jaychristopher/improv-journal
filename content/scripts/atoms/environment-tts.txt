[curious] Everyone says "establish the where." What is the where, actually?

[teaching] It's everything the scene is happening inside of that isn't a person. The room, the weather, the objects, how far apart you're standing, and — this is the one people forget — the time. A closing restaurant. A train leaving. The clock is part of the room.

[matter-of-fact] No. That's the mistake. A backdrop sits behind you and does nothing. The environment does things to you.

[teaching] Like handing out status. Think about it — the person behind the desk outranks the person standing in front of it. That's not character work, that's furniture. [contemplative] Huh. The room is playing.

[emphatic] The room is playing. And it's generating offers the whole time. Every object, every wall, every temperature is a latent offer just sitting there waiting for someone to pick it up. You are never out of material in a fully realized space. You're out of material in a void.
