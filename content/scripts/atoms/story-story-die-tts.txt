[teaching] It's removing your turn. That's the whole thing. In a story circle, you can see your turn coming — three people away, two people away, and you've got maybe fifteen seconds to prep a sentence. [curious] Mid-word. Not between sentences.

[emphatic] Mid-word. If you're handing over at a full stop you are playing a different, much easier game. The whole mechanism lives in that handover. [contemplative] So the only way to survive is to be composing the sentence the whole time. Whether or not you're the one saying it.

[teaching] Right. You're running the story in parallel with the person telling it. That's a strange and specific skill and it is much closer to what an actual scene demands than any circle exercise. In a scene nobody announces your turn either. Your partner says something and the next beat is yours, immediately, with no warning bell.

[curious] What does the elimination add?

[matter-of-fact] It makes hesitation audible. That's the honest case for it. In most exercises a pause is a soft failure — the energy dips a little, a coach might mention it in notes, might not. Here the pause is the entire event. You stop, you're out, and the whole room watched it happen. There's no ambiguity about what went wrong. It wasn't your idea. It was the gap before the idea.

[curious] And you judge that on grammar, not on whether the idea was any good.

[emphatic] This is the part people get wrong constantly. The rule is: did the sentence continue coherently. That's it. The moment you start eliminating somebody because their choice was boring, you've taught the entire room to search for interesting choices — and searching takes time, and time is exactly what the game punishes. You've built a machine that demands two contradictory things and then eliminates people for failing at both.

[contemplative] So the conductor's job is narrower than it looks.

[teaching] Much narrower. Point mid-word. Judge grammar. Take a suggestion for the title only — not the setting, not the character, not the genre. Every extra constraint is another target players are trying to hit, and aiming is the enemy here. Give them a title and let the story go wherever the listening takes it.
