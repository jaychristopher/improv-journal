[curious] You said the model has been revised. How badly?

[teaching] Not badly, but meaningfully, and in a direction that matters for us. Kahneman's version is a single pool — one tank of attention, everything drinks from it. Wickens came along later and argued it's more like several partially separate pools. Visual, auditory, spatial, verbal.

[curious] Why does that change anything?

[teaching] Because the single-pool model predicts that any two demanding tasks interfere with each other. And that's just not what happens. [emphatic] The 1973 model has no way to tell those two cases apart. It looks at both and says: high load, expect interference. It's right about the first one and wrong about the second.
