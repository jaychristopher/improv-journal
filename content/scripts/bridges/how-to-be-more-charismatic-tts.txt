[teaching] [pause] That's the mistake. Johnstone's insight was never that high status wins. It's that the socially powerful people are the fluid ones. [conversational] Charisma is having both channels running at once, and it's rare because they fight each other under pressure. [curious] Does any of this change in a group? Because "give one person your total attention" in a group of six sounds like a way to make five people feel excluded.

[teaching] It is exactly that. One-to-one, presence is all of you aimed at one person. In a group, that same move reads as a door closing. [emphatic] Which means group charisma is about distribution, not performance. The person who brings the quiet one in, or credits somebody's point from ten minutes ago, is doing something more charismatic than the person telling the best story. And it's considerably easier.
