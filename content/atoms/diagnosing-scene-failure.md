A procedure for turning "that scene didn't work" into something you can practise. Most improvisers plateau because they answer a bad scene with volume — more classes, more reps — when the useful question is which part broke.

## The problem with symptoms

"It was slow." "It felt forced." "We weren't connecting." These describe the experience of the failure, not its mechanism, and none of them tell you what to do on Tuesday. A diagnosis has to name something specific enough to have a drill attached to it.

## Step 1 — Name the collapse mode

`systemic-collapse-modes` gives three, and they are distinguishable from the outside:

- **Latency** — the rhythm lagged. Responses arrived late, the energy dropped. Recover with `latency-recovery`.
- **Fracture** — you were in different scenes. Someone's reality was denied or missed. Recover with `fracture-recovery`.
- **Decay** — nothing accumulated. Pleasant, busy, weightless; established facts stopped mattering. Recover with `decay-recovery`.

Modes cascade, so name the *first* one. A scene that ends in decay often began with latency, and drilling decay will not fix it.

## Step 2 — Find the shadow

Each collapse mode is produced by a behaviour, and each of those behaviours is a principle inverted. This is the step that converts a mode into a practice:

| Behaviour | Shadow of | Usually produces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `internal-computation` | Be Present | Latency |
| `hesitation` | Be Brave | Latency |
| `judgment` | Be Thankful | Latency |
| `negation` | Be Positive | Fracture |
| `bulldozing` | Be Supportive | Fracture |
| `steering` | Be Changeable | Fracture |
| `overcomplication` | Be Simple | Decay |
| `performing-cleverness` | Be Honest | Decay |

`blocking` is the general case rather than a row of its own — it is the family name for refusing what was offered, and it shows up as negation, steering or bulldozing depending on how the refusal is delivered.

## Step 3 — Practise the principle, not the symptom

The output of a diagnosis is a principle to rehearse. "Be less awkward" is not practisable. "I retreated into my head at the top of the scene, which is Be Present, so I am drilling first responses this week" is.

## Why it plateaus without this

Volume improves whatever you already do. If the failure is latency, more reps means more repetitions of computing before speaking, and the habit hardens. Diagnosis is what makes practice compound instead of merely accumulate.

## Attribution note

the three-step procedure — mode, then shadow, then principle — is this site's synthesis rather than a published method. Its parts are not: the collapse modes come from `systemic-collapse-modes`, and the shadow relation between each antipattern and its principle is how the atoms here are already organised. Napier's *Improvise* is the nearest published relative, in treating scene failure as analysable rather than a matter of talent. **Counter-position (Napier, and Johnstone by implication):** naming failure modes can feed the self-monitoring that causes latency in the first place. This is a tool for after the scene — for coaching, notes and practice planning — and using it while performing is likely to cause the problem it diagnoses.
