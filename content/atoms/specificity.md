Naming the particular rather than the category. Not a dog but a whippet with a limp. Not your job but the Tuesday shift at the garden centre. Specificity is the cheapest move available in a scene and the one most consistently skipped.

## What a specific detail actually does

Three things at once, none of which a general one manages.

It gives your partner material. "A dog" offers nothing to build on. "A whippet with a limp" arrives with a temperament, a history and a reason somebody cares, and every one of those is a handle.

It characterises the speaker. Only a person who knows about whippets says whippet. The detail describes whoever chose it as much as the thing itself, which is why specificity does relationship work while appearing to do description.

It settles instantly. Nobody can dispute a detail they have just been given, so it becomes shared reality the moment it is said — a fast, cheap addition to `base-reality` that costs no negotiation at all.

## The failure it repairs

Generic scenes. Two people agreeing pleasantly about nothing in particular, in a location that has no features, about a subject neither has committed to. `decay-recovery` names the remedy directly: when a scene has drifted from specific to abstract, one concrete detail restores it faster than any amount of added energy, and considerably faster than a new plot.

That is also why the note "be more specific" outperforms almost every other note a coach can give. It is actionable in the next sentence, and it requires no analysis of what went wrong.

## Detail is not the same as specificity

The common misapplication is volume. Piling adjectives onto a noun is decoration, it costs bandwidth, and it usually makes an offer harder to catch rather than easier — one exact noun beats three approximate ones.

Worse is specificity aimed at the wrong thing. Elaborate backstory delivered in the first minute is highly specific and almost always unhelpful, because it commits the scene to material nobody asked for and leaves less room than the vagueness it replaced.

## Where it comes from

Not invention. Recall. The reliable route to a specific detail is to use a real one — an actual street, an actual brand, an actual small annoyance you have had — which is why `obvious-choice` produces specificity as a by-product: the first thing that occurs to you is usually remembered rather than constructed, and remembered things come with detail already attached.

This is the mechanism behind the claim that honesty expresses itself as specificity. `be-honest` is not asking for confession. It is asking you to use what you actually know, and what you actually know is specific.
