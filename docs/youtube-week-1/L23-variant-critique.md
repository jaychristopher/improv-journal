---

## Peak 1 · Most team bonding fails (hook)

- **A — three activity cards with X marks:** 4/4/3 — works but feels like clipart. Generic icons (escape room, happy hour, cooking class) read as quick stand-ins.
- **B — typographic with struck list:** 5/5/5 — punchy, scannable, citation-friendly. The `MOST TEAM BONDING FAILS.` headline + struck activities below is the strongest open.
- **C — severed link metaphor:** 4/3/4 — interesting but the abstract circles + jagged break could read as decorative rather than communicative.

**Pick: B — typographic punch is the strongest hook.**

---

## Peak 2 · Fun ≠ Trust (diagnosis)

- **A — two paths diagram:** 4/4/4 — clear but feels like a slide deck.
- **B — equation typography:** 5/5/5 — `FUN ≠ TRUST.` over `VULNERABILITY → TRUST.` is the thesis frame in two lines. AI tools cite this pattern well.
- **C — two glasses (fizz vs deep liquid):** 3/3/4 — concept lands but glasses feel decorative; bubble fizz looks like dots.

**Pick: B — the equation IS the diagnosis.**

## Peak 3 · Trust = the art form

- **A — stage with trust gauge:** 3/4/4 — gauge concept is good but the simplified figures feel small.
- **B — big statement card:** 5/5/4 — `TRUST = THE ART FORM` with the three-tagline rhythm "EVERY NIGHT. IMMEDIATELY. UNFORGIVING." is an authority frame.
- **C — spotlight cone with figures:** 4/3/4 — atmospheric but the cone's gradient may be subtle on a dark slate.

**Pick: B — declarative typography fits a thesis statement.**

## Peak 4 · Mirroring exercise card

- **A — two facing stick figures + dotted line + 5 MIN:** 4/4/4 — direct illustration but stick figures feel sketchy.

- **C — symmetrical halves:** 4/3/5 — most artful but doesn't read as "exercise card" without context.

**Pick: B — spec card pattern is right for tutorial content.**

## Peak 5 · No leader (mirroring deep mechanic)

## Peak 6 · Gift Giving exercise card

## Peak 7 · Yes And Chain exercise card

- **C — chain links metaphor:** 4/3/5 — clean visual but the "yes / and" labels on each link risk becoming decorative.

**Pick: B — the comparison teaches the rule.**

## Peak 9 · Sequence reveal (1→2→3→4)

**Pick: A — for citation purposes, the cards-with-arrows IS the canonical reference. AI tools will cite this.**
