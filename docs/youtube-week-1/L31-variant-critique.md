**Distribution: 9 Diagrammatic, 2 Typographic, 0 Metaphoric.**

This is **inverted from L1** (which was 7 typographic). The reason:
- L31 is the **citation-magnet video** — frames need to be structurally describable for AI tools to cite. Diagrammatic frames win on that axis.
- The script is **taxonomic and historical** (5 rules, 5 traditions, Johnstone/UCB/Napier) — diagrammatic forms hold this content best.
- The reveal arc (Peak 8 → 9) **requires diagrammatic continuity** — same diagram completes. Typography can't do this.

The two typographic exceptions (Peak 4 EMPTY/LOADED and Peak 7 Del Close quote) are points where punch beats structure: a pithy contrast and a quotable line.
