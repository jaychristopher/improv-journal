## SOP 01 · Persona + JTBD (adapted)

### Persona brainstorm (6)

1. **Mid-career professional in a stuck negotiation** — boss, peer, or report won't budge; they've tried the direct argument and it failed
2. **Partner / spouse in a recurring argument** — same fight, different week; aware that confrontation isn't working
3. **Parent of a teenager** — direct opposition consistently backfires; wants the same outcome via a different route
4. **Activist / advocate** — has the facts, watches them bounce off the audience; wants to know why and what to do
5. **Manager trying to influence sideways** — no positional authority over the person whose mind needs to move
6. **Sales / fundraising / BD professional** — runs into ideological resistance that price + features don't crack

### Primary persona

**The stuck-conversation pro** — someone who has *already had* the hard conversation, tried the direct frontal approach, watched it fail, and now suspects the problem isn't their argument's quality but its delivery. Specifically: a manager-or-IC in their 30s-40s with skin in repeated high-stakes conversations (partner, report, parent, peer) where the same disagreement keeps recurring.

**Why this slice over single-event persona:**

- Single-event personas ("I have to tell my landlord X tomorrow") want a script, not a model. The thread doesn't give scripts; it gives an architecture for *why* scripts fail.
- The recurring-conversation persona is past the "give me three steps" phase. They've already done three steps and lost. They're ready to hear about belief architecture and framing.
- Returning-need vs one-time crisis: this persona will come back for the next conversation, and the next. Builds the channel's compounding-audience flywheel.

### JTBD — 4 lenses

- **Functional:** Move someone — partner, report, peer, family — on a position they've defended in past conversations, without triggering the defense reflex that has worked against me before
- **Emotional:** Stop feeling helpless in conversations where I'm right and still can't get heard; restore a sense that I can actually influence outcomes through dialogue
- **Social:** Be seen as the person who navigates conflict skillfully — neither pushy nor passive — and whose presence in a hard conversation makes things better, not worse
- **Anti-job:** No manipulation tactics, no "100 sneaky persuasion tricks," no salesy framework with a five-letter acronym, no patronizing "active listening is when you really, truly hear them." I want a model, not a script

### Persona expectations

- **Length:** 6-9 min — this is a model, not a tactic; needs more time than a "3 tips" video but should not be a 25-min lecture
- **Format:** principle + named mechanism + example — three pieces (framing / stakes / role) with one worked example each
- **Tone:** structural, not motivational; named the mechanism the persona already half-suspects but never had words for
- **Examples vs theory:** 60/40 toward examples — every named mechanism gets a recognizable real-world scene
- **Time investment:** watch once, recognize three things from their own life, apply on the next conversation

### SERP white-space angle (PROVISIONAL — see SOP 02 below)

> **The conversation isn't an argument; it's an architecture problem.**

Direct argument fails because beliefs are defended as architecture, not preferences. The persona has already tried — and failed — the standard "direct, honest, well-reasoned" approach. The unique angle is naming *why* it failed and giving a layered model: model their model humbly, work the edges not the core, frame the angle of approach.

This differs from the SERP for L17's keyword ("how to have difficult conversations") which is dominated by Crucial Conversations / Difficult Conversations book-summary content + therapist-style scripts. The thread's content is **structural** ("here's why your argument bounced") where the SERP is **procedural** ("here's a 4-step framework").

### Three anchor insights (each cited)

**1. Beliefs are defended as architecture, not preference — surprise minimization keeps the structure standing.**
- Source: Friston, "The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?" (2010, *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 11); Festinger, *A Theory of Cognitive Dissonance* (1957); Kahan et al., "Motivated Numeracy and Enlightened Self-Government" (2017, *Behavioural Public Policy* 1)
- Why load-bearing: defangs "they're being stubborn" — recasts the defense as structural, not character

**2. Rigid core, malleable edge — reactance fires at the core; the edge is where movement happens.**
- Source: Brehm, *A Theory of Psychological Reactance* (1966); Tversky & Kahneman, "Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases" (1974, *Science* 185); Cialdini, *Influence* (1984) — consistency principle
- Why load-bearing: the *practical* lever — gives the persona a place to actually push that isn't "stop talking"

**3. Framing isn't manipulation, it's recognition that meaning is constituted in reception.**
- Source: Tversky & Kahneman, "The Framing of Decisions and the Psychology of Choice" (1981, *Science* 211); Lakoff, *Don't Think of an Elephant!* (2004); Johnstone, *Impro* — "be obvious" as response to partner-unknowability
- Why load-bearing: the channel-native bridge — improv has been training framing-as-skill for 60 years; this gives the channel its defensible angle vs. Lakoff-only or Cialdini-only takes

**Recommendation:** **Option B.** The thread provides exactly the structural-explanation layer that L17 needs to differentiate from Crucial Conversations / Difficult Conversations book-summary SERP. Writing the L17 script with this thread as conceptual backbone is one move that resolves three friction points (no new keyword needed, no new bridge needed, no new channel-plan entry needed).

## What this run revealed (preview — full gap doc separate)

1. **Channel-plan lookup fails at step 1.** Concept-level content (threads, undeveloped atoms) has no entry. The existing SOP 01 assumes the upstream triage already happened.
2. **Output file location convention is ambiguous.** L4 lives at `docs/youtube-week-3/L4-...-buildup.md`. This thread is not in a youtube-week batch. Wrote to `docs/concepts/shaping-shared-reality-buildup.md` as a proposed new convention.
3. **Keyword scan is a missing SOP step.** SOP 02 assumes the keyword was chosen in SOP 01 step 1 (from the channel plan). When that input doesn't exist, there's no documented procedure for picking a candidate. Surfaced naturally as "Step 0 — Keyword scan" above.
4. **SERP intent check is implicit, not enforced.** SOP 02 step 3 says "Identify white-space gaps" but doesn't formally check whether the SERP intent matches informational-content intent. The Carnegie-domination of "how to influence people" should have been a fast fail; instead it took 928 units of API spend to discover.
5. **Thread → video routing is undocumented.** The thread is content-complete and ready to back L17, but there's no formal step that says "use thread X as the conceptual backbone of script Y." This is the missing SOP A-04 (Bridge promotion) or A-05 (Channel plan intake).
