# Reference · 30 Engagement Rules

The 30 rules synthesized from MrBeast (employee handbook), Veritasium, Kurzgesagt, Ali Abdaal, Mark Rober, and MKBHD. This is the canonical list. Other SOPs reference it.

## Hook & first 30 seconds

1. **Hook in 3 seconds** — first sentence is a counterintuitive claim or specific stake; ≤ 8 spoken words
2. **Payoff before earned** — the reframe / answer lands inside first 15 seconds
3. **Title + thumbnail inseparable** — the thumbnail completes a thought the title started
4. **Curiosity gap** — name the thing the viewer doesn't know but wants to
5. **Stakes in 15s** — explicit cost named, not implied
6. **No channel intro** — first 5 seconds is value, not branding

## Pacing

7. **Cut every 3-7s** — visual change cadence; even talking-head needs B-roll punctuation
8. **Re-hook every 30-60s** — visible cadence change every minute or less
9. **Cuts on verbal pauses** — ride the natural breath/comma; don't cut mid-word
10. **Vary shot scale** — mix close, medium, wide; same scale for 30s+ feels static
11. **Sound design as punctuation** — small "whoosh" / "tick" on cuts adds energy
12. **Speed-ramp at structural breaks** — micro speed-up between sections signals new beat
13. **One visual surprise per chapter** — every section earns one "huh, didn't expect that"

## Narrative

14. **Open loops** — specific thing promised in first 30s, resolved later
15. **Midpoint reveal** — most quotable line lands at ~50%, isolated by pauses
16. **3-act structure** — hook → mechanism+moves → antipattern+close
17. **CTA after value** — URL only at end
18. **Specificity** — named teachers + years + numbers + 2-3 worked examples
19. **End on callback** — last line echoes opening hook

## Visual

20. **Faces** — human faces hold attention better than abstract visuals (use sparingly given the channel's typography-first style)
21. **Concept-first text** — large headline conveys the idea; supporting copy supports
22. **Color-coded concepts** — red=problem, orange=tension, green=resolution
23. **Animation matches speech** — element appears as it's spoken, not before
24. **Negative space** — don't fill every pixel; breathing room earns attention
25. **Captions as design** — typography style is part of the channel signature

## Audio / voice

26. **Conversational delivery** — second-person "you", contractions, no academic register
27. **Dynamic range** — multiple [emote] tags varied across beats
28. **Silence is louder than music** — strategic silence beats generic background

## Channel-level

29. **One question per video** — title states it, video resolves it; no multi-thesis bundles
30. **Thumbnail is the product** — viewer judges the channel by thumbnail before they watch a frame
