# SOP 01 · Persona + Jobs To Be Done

## Purpose

Identify who the video is for and what they're trying to accomplish before any research, script, or visual decision. Skipping this produces well-made content that misses its audience.

## Inputs

- The chosen video from the channel plan (e.g. L4 = "How to Deal with Rejection")
- Channel plan score sheet (`docs/youtube-channel-plan.md` — section 8 "Production Priority")
- Knowledge of the channel's pillar map (P1 mental game / P2 conversation / P3 team / P4 practice / P5 theory)

## Outputs

- `docs/youtube-week-N/<slug>-buildup.md` — single doc that captures every iteration's work for this video, starting with persona + JTBD section.

## Tools

- Read tool to inspect channel plan and any existing context
- Manual reasoning. No external API needed.

## Steps

1. **Look up the video in the channel plan.** Confirm: keyword, search volume, KD, pillar, score, slug.
2. **Brainstorm 4-6 personas** who might type the keyword. Don't filter yet. For "how to be funny" we generated: socially anxious 20-something, dating-aged person, professional networker, aspiring creator, student/teen, parent/teacher.
3. **Pick the primary persona.** Criteria:
   - Largest search-volume slice of the keyword
   - Strongest emotional pull (rejection > status > learning)
   - Mass-market resonance (other personas can pull from same content)
4. **Write the JTBD in 4 lenses:**
   - **Functional:** what the user wants done in tactical terms ("Make people laugh at things I say")
   - **Emotional:** what feeling they're chasing ("Feel like the person others want around")
   - **Social:** how they want to be seen ("The kind of person invited to things")
   - **Anti-job:** what they DON'T want from this content ("Don't make me memorize jokes")
5. **List what the persona expects** when they search. Length, format, tone, examples-vs-theory mix, time investment.
6. **Identify the white-space angle** — what no one in the SERP is saying that matches your channel's POV. This becomes the script's hook.
7. **Pick 3 anchor insights** from the bridge that the script will be built around. Each insight needs a citable source.

## Quality bar

- Primary persona named, not "general audience"
- 4 JTBD lenses written, not just functional
- White-space angle is concretely different from top SERP results (you've actually read them in iter 2)
- 3 anchor insights named, each with a source (named teacher + year minimum)

## Common pitfalls

- **Persona too generic.** "People who want to be funnier" isn't a persona. "Socially anxious 20-something who freezes in groups" is.
- **Skipping the anti-job.** Without naming what the persona DOESN'T want, you'll add filler.
- **Picking insights without sources.** If you can't name the teacher and year, the insight is hearsay and the script loses citation magnetism.

## Estimated time

15-30 minutes. The work is mostly thinking, not typing.

## Lessons from prior production

- L1: confirmed bandwidth angle is the white space — every other "stop overthinking" article is willpower-framed
- L2: confirmed "stop trying" is the white space — every other "how to be funny" is "do this list"
- L23: confirmed "vulnerability over fun" is the white space — every other "team building" is activity-listicle
- L31: confirmed "half are wrong" is the white space — every other "rules of improv" parrots the same 5 rules
