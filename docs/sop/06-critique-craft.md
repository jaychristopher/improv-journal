# SOP 06 · Critique Round 2 — Best-Practice Craft

## Purpose

Audit v1 against successful YouTubers' specific craft principles. Round 1 (SOP 05) was rule-based; this round is taste-based — the patterns that distinguish "B+ retention" from "A retention".

## Inputs

- Script v1 from SOP 04
- Retention scores from SOP 05

## Outputs

A "craft audit" section in the buildup doc with:
- Per-source verdict (✓ / refine / break)
- Cross-cutting v2 edits not surfaced by SOP 05
- Combined edit list (SOP 05 + SOP 06) for v2

## Reference patterns

### MrBeast (leaked employee handbook)

- Every line earns the next. Cut filler ruthlessly.
- Payoff in shot 1 — the climax shows up first.
- Specificity beats generality. "47 ways" > "many ways".

### Veritasium (Derek Muller)

- Open loops with sustained tension, not just early teases.
- The reveal earns its setup. Bridge from setup to reveal explicitly.
- Counterintuitive framings dominate.
- Show, don't tell — concrete demonstrations win over narration.

### Kurzgesagt

- Build complexity gradually, never assume prior knowledge.
- Single thesis enforced — every beat reinforces.
- Visual cadence implied in script — each beat is a visual moment.

### Ali Abdaal

- Conversational warmth, second-person discipline.
- "Let me tell you" / "here's what I do" framing.
- Authentic confessional moments soften authority.

### Mark Rober

- Stakes named early and big.
- Specific consequences ("I tested this with 1,000 people").
- The setup IS the entertainment.

### MKBHD

- No hype words ("amazing", "incredible", "you won't believe").
- Concrete claims with sources.
- Confident matter-of-fact tone.

## Steps

1. **For each source above, ask:** Does v1 do this? Mark ✓ / refine / break.
2. **Look for craft gaps** that SOP 05's rule-based audit didn't catch:
   - Throat-clearing first sentences (MrBeast cut)
   - Bridges between sections that earn the reveal (Veritasium)
   - Missing complexity gradient (Kurzgesagt)
   - Missing personal moments (Ali Abdaal)
   - Hype words slipping in (MKBHD)
3. **Combine the SOP 05 + SOP 06 edit lists.** Deduplicate and sort by ROI.
4. **Write the craft section** of the buildup doc with: per-source verdict table, combined edit list, total expected length change.

## Quality bar

- All 6 sources audited
- 2-3 craft-specific edits surfaced beyond SOP 05's rule edits
- Combined edit list ready for v2 implementation
- Hype-word check explicitly performed (MKBHD principle)

## Common pitfalls

- **Treating this as duplicate of SOP 05.** It's not. SOP 05 is rule-based ("does it have a hook?"). SOP 06 is craft-based ("does the bridge from setup to reveal feel earned?").
- **Skipping the bridge check.** Veritasium's distinguishing trait is HOW the reveal arrives, not whether there is one. v1 often jumps to the science detour without a bridge. Add a one-line foreshadow ("There's a switch in your brain.").
- **Adding hype to "fix" weak claims.** If a claim feels weak, don't say "amazing" — make the claim more specific.

## Estimated time

20-30 minutes.

## Lessons from prior production

- L2 v1 had no bridge from "stop trying" reframe to the Limb fMRI study — felt like a science detour. v2 added: "There's a switch in your brain that explains why trying kills it."
- L1 v2 fixed a Veritasium gap: explicit bridge from "internal computation" to "redirect attention" with a specific anchor.
- Across all 4 videos, removing 2-4 throat-clearing words tightened hooks materially. Watch for "If you want to..." / "Have you ever..." / "Let me tell you about..." in opening sentences.
- No hype words slipped in across the 4 — the bridge content's academic register protected against it. Keep this discipline.
