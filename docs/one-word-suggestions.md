# One word for a longform opening — the draft list

_Drafted 2026-09-30 for PG-2.1. A hundred words for the owner's teacher to
cut to about eighty. Nothing here is in the generator yet; PG-2.2 builds the kind
from whatever survives._

## Why a single word, when the guide says a bare noun is the weakest start

For a two-person scene it is. A longform opening is different: a Harold or an
Armando takes one word from the crowd and the scenes come from three minutes
of association, not from the word itself, so the word's job is to give the
group something to associate from. What works is a common noun with a smell,
a weight or a history — something everyone in the room has held — over an
abstraction or a joke. A word with two meanings gives the pattern game a
second leaf. "Banana" gives nothing.

Marks: **(p)** lands on a life and stays out of a school room; **(a)** needs
adult knowledge and stays out of a school room too. Everything else is open
to every room.

## Things with a smell or a weight

- rust — every garage has some, and it says time passed
- varnish — a smell and a job half done
- vinegar — sharp, kitchens and chips and cleaning
- chalk — a classroom, a cliff, a pavement drawing
- sawdust — a workshop, a circus floor, a pub
- mildew — a tent, a cellar, a forgotten bag
- diesel — a bus station, a boat, a long journey
- tar — hot roads, roofs, a smell from childhood
- wax — candles, a surfboard, a sealed letter
- leather — a jacket, a saddle, a chair that was someone's
- wool — a jumper someone knitted, an itch, a farm
- ink — a stain, a signature, a pen that leaked
- linen — a cupboard, a wedding, a hospital bed
- rope — a swing, a boat, a tug of war
- gravel — a drive, a path, a sound at night
- plaster — a wall, a cast, a ceiling coming down
- cardboard — a move, a fort, a sign
- foil — a kitchen, a hat, something kept warm

## Places

- cellar — what is kept down there
- attic — what was put up there and forgotten
- garage — the car, the bench, the thing that never got fixed
- basement — the flat below, the band, the flood
- porch — the wait, the delivery, the conversation before going in
- hallway — where people pass and do not stop
- pantry — what is running out
- greenhouse — heat, glass, something growing that should not
- kennel — the dog's, or the place the dog was left
- chapel — small, quiet, somebody's wedding or somebody's end (p)
- locker — school, a gym, a station; the key that was lost
- warehouse — scale, boxes, a night shift
- lobby — a hotel, an office, the waiting before the meeting
- pier — the end of it, the wind, the arcade
- quarry — the swimming nobody was allowed, the drop
- orchard — the picking, the ladder, the wasps
- reservoir — the drowned village, the walk round it
- stairwell — echo, cigarettes, the place to cry at work

## Paper and institutions

- inheritance — who got it and who did not (p)
- deposit — a bank, a bottle, a river; three meanings at once
- invoice — the job done and the wait to be paid (a)
- receipt — proof, or the lack of it
- lease — the flat, the year, the clause nobody read (a)
- passport — a photo, a queue, a name that is not quite yours
- warranty — the thing that broke the week after
- permit — the thing you were not allowed to do
- ledger — what is owed, written down
- pension — the sum, the wait, the letter (a)
- mortgage — the house and its weight (a)
- custody — the child, the keys, the object (p)
- verdict — the room goes quiet
- apprenticeship — the first year, the master, the mistake
- audition — the wait outside, the one line
- inventory — counting what is left
- refund — the argument at the counter
- souvenir — the thing that was worth it once
- heirloom — the thing you cannot get rid of

## Objects with a history

- compass — the walk, the direction argued over
- thermos — a flask on a cold morning, a sideline, a night shift
- harmonica — a pocket, a campfire, one tune
- typewriter — the letter that took all night
- lantern — a tent, a power cut, a search
- suitcase — arriving, leaving, the one that was lost
- doorbell — the wait after ringing it
- thermostat — the household war
- padlock — the shed, the bike, the diary
- ladder — the gutter, the apples, the fall
- kettle — the wait, the offer, the last thing in the flat
- mattress — the move, the floor, the guest
- radiator — the cold room, the coins to feed it, the argument
- wheelbarrow — the garden, the child pushed in it
- toolbox — whose it was, what is missing from it
- trophy — the shelf, the dust, the year it was won
- splinter — small, sharp, somebody with tweezers
- envelope — sealed, addressed, not yet opened
- postcard — sent late, arrived after
- hourglass — the turn, the last grains
- lampshade — the party, the wrong colour
- scaffolding — the building you cannot see, the shout from above

## Two meanings

- draft — a cold air, a first version, a beer, a call-up
- settlement — a village, an agreement, the ground sinking
- tender — sore, an offer, a boat, a feeling
- bolt — a lock, a run, lightning, a roll of cloth
- dock — a harbour, a court, a pay cut
- jam — the traffic, the jar, the music
- stall — the market, the engine, the delay
- toll — the road, the bell, the cost
- vault — the bank, the jump, the ceiling
- wake — the funeral, the boat, the morning (p)

## Weather and food

- frost — the first one, the car, the garden gone
- thaw — after the freeze, between two people
- drought — the hosepipe, the cracked ground
- harvest — the field, the festival, what was gathered
- curfew — the hour, the parent, the town
- marmalade — bitter, breakfast, somebody's grandmother's
- gravy — a Sunday, a boat of it, the argument about lumps
- leftovers — the fridge, the day after the party
- rhubarb — the garden, the crumble, the word actors mutter
- hiccup — small, involuntary, at the worst moment
- whisper — what could not be said aloud
- blister — the walk, the shoe, the day after (p)
- detour — the road closed, the long way round

## Avoided on purpose

Atlantic splits: licence, tinfoil, petrol, tarpaulin, draught, queue. Words
the site's autolinker would turn into a link inside the guide's run because
they are atom titles: opening, editing, status, trust. Grim for a hall:
ashes, grave, prison.
