# MZ — Monetization

`docs/monetization.md` (2026-09-27) is the ruling: at two hundred visitors a
month nothing here earns more than pocket money, the one fool-proof mechanism
is already built and switched off, and the number that decides revenue is
visitors. This epic holds the steps that follow from that ruling and nothing
speculative.

## Standing rule for this epic

Nothing goes on a page that the reader did not come for. A buy button on a
book page is the page's own decision made purchasable; an advertisement is not.
Every card here has to say what it earns at today's traffic, in the record's
numbers, before it says what it might earn.
