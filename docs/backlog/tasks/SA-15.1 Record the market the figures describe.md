# SA-15.1 — Record the market

## What was found

**Clicks by country, GSC via Ahrefs, 2026-04-01 → 2026-09-25:**

| Country                         | Clicks | Share |
| ------------------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| United States                   | **16** | 44%   |
| Great Britain                   | 5      | 14%   |
| Australia                       | 5      | 14%   |
| India                           | 3      | 8%    |
| Mexico, Germany                 | 2 each | 11%   |
| Singapore, Czechia, New Zealand | 1 each | 8%    |

Around a hundred further countries appear with impressions and no clicks.

**Volume by country, for two of the site's core terms:**

| Keyword         | US    | Global (top 12) | US share |
| --------------- | ----- | --------------- | -------- |
| `improv games`  | 3,100 | ~4,250          | 73%      |
| `theatre games` | 1,400 | ~1,850          | 76%      |

**And nothing in the repo records which market its numbers come from.** Every
`volume`, `difficulty` and `traffic_potential` across 317 declared keywords was
retrieved `country=us`, as was every figure in every card of this epic.
CLAUDE.md's SEO discipline section names every field and the rule against
inventing numbers, and never names a country. `schema.ts` has no market field.
`PROMOTION_FLOOR = 10_000` is therefore a US-only threshold sitting roughly a
third below the global demand it is trying to measure.

## Read the click numbers carefully

Thirty-six clicks is a small sample and this card does not pretend otherwise.
"Great Britain is 14%" rests on five clicks and would move on one. Do not build
a market strategy on it.

What the sample does support is narrower and sufficient: **the audience is
plainly not US-only.** Twenty of 36 clicks from outside the United States is not
a rounding artefact, and it is enough to say that treating the US as the whole
market is a choice rather than a fact — which is exactly what nothing in the
repo currently says.

## One thing checked and found clean

The `theatre` / `theater` spelling is **not** a market signal, and an earlier
guess of mine that it was is wrong. "Theatre games" is 76% US-searched — 1,400
of about 1,850 — so Americans use the British spelling for this term too. The
site's choice of `theatre-games` costs nothing in the US and the guide already
declares both spellings with the shared parent `theater games`. Leave it alone.

## Run

1. **Declare the market in the schema.** Add it where the keyword figures live,
   defaulting to `us`, so a figure carries the market it was measured in. The
   repo already treats absence as meaningful for `serp_verdict`; do the same
   here rather than letting an undeclared country be assumed.
2. **Say it in CLAUDE.md.** The SEO discipline section is where somebody learns
   the rules before touching a number. One sentence — these figures are US
   unless declared otherwise, and a refresh must use the same market — prevents
   the whole class of error.
3. **Note the global multiplier next to the thresholds, do not change them.**
   `PROMOTION_FLOOR` and `STRANDED_DIFFICULTY` are defensible as US thresholds.
   Record in their comment blocks that US is roughly 73–76% of global volume for
   these terms, so the effective global floor is about 13,000. Changing the
   constants belongs to SA-4.1, which owns the promotion block.
4. **Do not refetch 317 keywords.** That is expensive, it would churn every
   guide's frontmatter, and nothing in this card needs it. If a global view is
   wanted later, sample a dozen terms and record the ratio.
5. **Leave hreflang alone.** A single-locale English site serving a global
   English audience does not need it, and adding it would be a large change
   chasing a five-click signal.

## Verify

- The keyword schema carries a market, defaulting to `us`, and the guard for
  frontmatter shape covers it.
- CLAUDE.md's SEO section states the market rule.
- `top-guides.ts` and `seo-audit.mjs` comment blocks record the US share of
  global volume, dated.
- No guide's declared figures changed.
- No hreflang was added.
- `npm run check` green.

## Scoring

- **impact 3** — it corrects a systematic and knowable bias in every threshold
  the repo prioritises with, and it closes a whole class of future error where a
  refreshed number silently describes a different market. Not 4: it moves no
  page and brings no visitor, and the click evidence that motivates it is 36
  clicks, which the epic's own rule says should cap enthusiasm. Not 2: the
  volume-share measurement is solid and independent of the small sample, and the
  undeclared-market defect is real regardless of who is clicking.
- **radius 4** — 317 keyword declarations across 78 guides, two threshold
  constants, the audit's buckets and CLAUDE.md's stated discipline. Not 5: it
  changes no atom, no URL and no content, and SA-10.1's question about what
  those fields mean when absent is the wider one.
- **opportunity 12**
- **complexity 2** — a schema field with a default, one paragraph of
  documentation, and two dated comments. Steps 4 and 5 exist to forbid the
  expensive interpretations, and with them the work is small and entirely
  reversible. Not 1: touching `schema.ts` is touching the declared authority for
  the content model, and a default that is wrong would be inherited silently by
  every future figure.
- **roi 6.0**

## Outcome

**Done 2026-09-25.** The market is declared where the figures live, the rule
is in CLAUDE.md, the two thresholds carry the global multiplier beside them
and keep their values, and no figure was refetched.

**Step 1 — `market` on `BridgeTargetKeyword`.** An optional Ahrefs country
code, absent meaning `us`, with the account in its doc comment: all 317
keywords on 78 guides read with `country=us`, the SERP readings beside them
likewise, 20 of 36 clicks from elsewhere, US about three quarters of global
volume on the two terms measured. No guide declares it today, which is
correct — declaring `us` 317 times would say nothing. The guard is
`keyword-market.test.ts`: a declared market must be a two-letter code, and no
guide may mix markets across its keywords, because the promotion block and
the audit compare those figures against each other. There is no runtime
validation of frontmatter to extend — gray-matter passes fields through and
`schema.ts` is the only authority — so the type and the guard are the whole
implementation.

**Step 2 — CLAUDE.md.** One bullet under the SEO discipline, after "Never
invent a number": every figure is the United States; read a figure in the
market it carries; never mix markets inside one guide.

**Step 3 — the multiplier, dated, values unchanged.** `PROMOTION_FLOOR`
records that 10,000 of US reach is roughly 13,000 of global demand and stays
a US threshold on purpose — the figures it compares against are US, and a
global floor against US figures would be the error. `STRANDED_DIFFICULTY`
records that difficulty is computed from the results page in the market it
was read for. `seo-audit.mjs` carries the same note above `WINNABLE_KD` and
`STRANDED_KD`.

**Steps 4 and 5.** Nothing refetched; no guide's figures changed — nothing
under `content/` is in the diff; no hreflang.

**What moved.** No content. Registers 19 done, 22 open; the SA-15 story
closes with its only task. `npm run check` green, 279 files, 1,448 tests.
