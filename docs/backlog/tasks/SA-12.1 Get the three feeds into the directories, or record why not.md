# SA-12.1 — Get the feeds onto a shelf

## What was found

Measured against production, 2026-09-25:

| Show                            | Episodes | Feed        | Apple link | Spotify link |
| ------------------------------- | -------- | ----------- | ---------- | ------------ |
| `/listen/improv-lab`            | **173**  | 200, 544 KB | none       | none         |
| `/listen/physics-of-connection` | **78**   | 200, 201 KB | none       | none         |
| `/listen/deep-cuts`             | **68**   | 200, 154 KB | none       | none         |

**319 episodes.** Each has an MP3, a duration, a transcript anchor on the page
it was made from, and `PodcastEpisode` structured data naming its series.

Across all 386 built pages there is **not one link** to Apple Podcasts,
Spotify, Pocket Casts, Overcast or the Podcast Index. The only Apple and
Spotify links in the entire build sit on `/library/carrane-improv-nerd` and the
library index, and they point at somebody else's show.

## Two things checked and found correct

Recorded so this card is not mistaken for a quality complaint.

- **The feeds are directory-compliant.** They declare
  `xmlns:itunes`, `xmlns:podcast` (Podcast Index) and `xmlns:atom`, carry an
  `atom:link rel="self"`, and include every tag Apple requires: `itunes:author`,
  three `itunes:category`, `itunes:image`, `itunes:explicit`, `itunes:owner` and
  `language`. There is nothing to fix before submitting.
- **The episode schema is right.** `PodcastEpisode` declares its audio as
  `associatedMedia` with `"@type": "MediaObject"` and a `contentUrl`, which is
  schema.org's own canonical shape for this type. An earlier reading of mine
  flagged the absence of `AudioObject` as a defect; it is not one, and the
  markup should be left alone.

## What is missing

Only distribution. The feed is reachable from the show page through a
`<link rel="alternate">` in the head and nothing else — the episode pages, which
are the guides and atoms themselves, carry no feed link at all.

It cannot be determined from the repository whether the shows have been
submitted to any directory. The absence of links is consistent with "never
submitted" and with "submitted but never linked", and those need different
fixes. Step 1 exists to find out which.

## A note on scope

This is podcast distribution rather than organic web search, and it is worth
saying so rather than filing it quietly under SEO. It belongs in this epic
because Apple Podcasts and Spotify are searched, because a listener who finds
an improv show about the mechanics of connection is the most qualified arrival
this site could get, and because the asset already exists at zero marginal
content cost. If the owner would rather not run a podcast, the right outcome is
to record that and stop building the feeds — which is also a useful answer.

## Run

1. **Find out whether the shows are already submitted.** Search Apple Podcasts
   and Spotify for each title. This is a login-and-search job, which is why the
   card is marked `human`. Record the answer either way; it decides everything
   below.
2. **If not submitted, submit.** Apple Podcasts Connect and Spotify for
   Creators both take the feed URL directly, and the feeds already pass their
   requirements. Podcast Index is free and needs no account. Do not change the
   feeds first — they are compliant.
3. **Link the directories from the show pages once the shows are live.** A
   listener on `/listen/improv-lab` currently has no way to subscribe in the app
   they actually use. Wait until the listings exist; a dead badge is worse than
   no badge.
4. **Put the feed link on the episode pages too.** Each guide and atom with
   audio is an episode page. A `<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml">`
   in the head costs nothing and is how feed readers and some crawlers find a
   show from any page of it.
5. **Do not create separate episode URLs.** The current design — the guide _is_
   the episode page, with a transcript anchor — is correct and avoids
   duplicating 319 pages of content. Nothing here should change that.

## Verify

- Whether each show is listed in Apple Podcasts and Spotify is recorded, with
  the date checked.
- If submitted during this task, the listing URLs are recorded.
- Show pages link to every directory the show is actually live in, and to none
  it is not.
- Episode pages carry a feed `<link rel="alternate">`.
- No new episode URLs were created, and the three feeds still return 200.
- `npm run check` green.

## Scoring

- **impact 3** — 319 episodes is the largest existing asset on this site and it
  currently has no discovery surface at all, so the work is distribution rather
  than production and the marginal content cost is zero. The audience is exactly
  the one the epic is trying to reach. Not 4: no number has been retrieved for
  what directory listing would bring, and the epic's rule caps impact where the
  return is unmeasured — the cost side is counted precisely, the upside is not.
  Not 2: unlike the AI-answer channel in SA-8.1, this channel's mechanics are
  well understood and the submission step has a high certainty of working.
- **radius 3** — three show pages, three feeds, and a head-level link on every
  page that has audio. Not 4: it changes no content, no URLs and no ranking
  logic, and the episode pages themselves are untouched by design.
- **opportunity 9**
- **complexity 2** — the feeds need no changes, so the repo-side work is a
  handful of links and a head tag. Not 1: steps 1 and 2 are account work that an
  agent cannot do, step 3 depends on step 2 landing first, and a directory badge
  pointing at a listing that does not exist is a worse state than today.
- **roi 4.5** — level with SA-7.1. Both are small, cheap, and mostly about
  making an asset that already exists actually reachable.
