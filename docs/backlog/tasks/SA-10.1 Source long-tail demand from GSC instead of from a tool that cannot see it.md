# SA-10.1 — Source long-tail demand from GSC

## What was found

The thirteen queries Search Console has surfaced, 2026-06-01 → 2026-09-25,
carry 35 impressions and 0 clicks. Ten of them, put back to Ahrefs
`keywords-explorer-overview` on 2026-09-25:

| Query                               | GSC position | Ahrefs volume | difficulty | traffic_potential | parent_topic     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ---------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| retrospective justification meaning | **10.0**     | **0**         | —          | —                 | —                |
| pattern break                       | **9.3**      | 60            | —          | —                 | —                |
| base reality meaning                | 50.0         | **0**         | —          | —                 | —                |
| keith sawyer group genius           | 43.0         | **0**         | —          | —                 | —                |
| stimulation performance             | 49.8         | 20            | —          | —                 | —                |
| what is pacing and leading          | 90.0         | 20            | —          | —                 | —                |
| improv practice exercises           | 60.0         | 20            | —          | —                 | —                |
| resistance blocking                 | 63.0         | 30            | —          | —                 | —                |
| beginner improv games               | 52.0         | 40            | 0          | 450               | improv exercises |
| space work                          | 29.0         | 100           | 42         | **7,500**         | **spaces**       |

> **Correction, 2026-09-25.** An earlier draft of this card called those 35
> impressions "the complete measured demand this site has ever captured". That
> is wrong by two orders of magnitude. `gsc-performance-history` for the same
> window returns **6,602 impressions and 33 clicks**; the per-query table
> exposes 0.5% of the impressions and none of the clicks, because GSC withholds
> queries below its privacy threshold. The finding below is unaffected — it is
> about the ten queries that _are_ visible and what Ahrefs knows about them —
> but the sample is a sample, not the total. See [[SA-14.1 Read the total, not
the sample]].

**Seven of ten have no `traffic_potential`, no `difficulty` and no
`parent_topic`.** Those are precisely the three fields every threshold in this
repository is defined on: `PROMOTION_FLOOR = 10_000` and `STRANDED_DIFFICULTY =
30` in `top-guides.ts`, the ranking and bucketing in `seo-audit.mjs`, and the
collision test CLAUDE.md calls the collision test.

A blank here means "below the tool's reporting floor", not "nobody searches
it". The repo currently has no way to express that difference, so a term the
site holds **position 10** for is indistinguishable, to every piece of
machinery here, from a term nobody wants.

**And a populated field can be worse than a blank.** "Space work" returns 7,500
traffic potential at difficulty 42 under the parent topic **"spaces"** — office
and workspace searches. The site ranks 29th on it for _improv_ space work. Any
future decision that treats that 7,500 as this page's opportunity would be
chasing a subject the page is not about, and `parent` is the field meant to
catch exactly that.

**A second probe, recorded so nobody repeats it.** Ahrefs
`keywords-explorer-matching-terms` was asked for improv craft vocabulary —
improv scene, improv character, improv technique, improv listening, scene work,
improv status — at volume ≥ 100 and difficulty ≤ 15. It returned **three
keywords, none of them about improv**. There is no undiscovered pile of craft
terms with measurable volume. The vocabulary the site teaches simply lives
below the floor, which is consistent with `seo-audit.mjs`'s own note that the
site's best positions sit "on terms with almost no volume".

That closes off "find bigger craft keywords" as a strategy and leaves the
realistic one: many small terms, which is what 205 atoms already are.

## Run

1. **Record the null rate, dated, next to the thresholds it undermines.**
   `top-guides.ts` and `seo-audit.mjs` both carry long comment blocks arguing
   their constants. Add the measurement to each: on 2026-09-25, 7 of the 10
   queries this site ranks for had no value in the field this constant tests.
   That is the minimum useful outcome of this card and it costs two comments.
2. **Read the GSC query table, not only the page table.** Nothing in the repo
   calls `gsc-keywords`. It is the only instrument that can see this site's
   demand, it is first-party, and it returns the query as well as the position.
   Add it to the audit's dated GSC block alongside the pages reading.
3. **Give absence a name in the schema.** A keyword with no `traffic_potential`
   is currently indistinguishable from one nobody has looked up. The repo
   already solved this shape once — `serp_verdict` absent means "nobody looked"
   and that is documented as the correct state. Do the same here rather than
   defaulting a blank to zero, which is what every current threshold does.
4. **Fix the one active trap.** Record `parent: "spaces"` against the space-work
   figure wherever it is used, or leave the traffic potential out entirely with
   a note. Do not let a 7,500 about office space sit unlabelled in a file that
   ranks pages by traffic potential.
5. **Do not rewrite the thresholds in this card.** `PROMOTION_FLOOR` and
   `STRANDED_DIFFICULTY` are defensible for the bridge layer, which does target
   head terms and does have the fields populated. The finding is that they were
   never meant to judge the long tail and currently do so by silence. Changing
   them belongs with SA-4.1, which already owns the promotion block.

## Verify

- Both threshold comment blocks carry the dated null-rate measurement.
- `npm run seo:audit` reports the GSC query list, with each query's position and
  whether Ahrefs has any figure for it.
- A keyword with no retrieved `traffic_potential` is distinguishable in the
  schema from one recorded as low.
- The space-work traffic potential is either labelled with its true parent or
  removed.
- `npm run check` green.
