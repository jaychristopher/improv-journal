Every entry that has surfaced is ≥726 words. Every entry <700 words has not.
The split is not gradual — there is a 211-word gap at the boundary, the largest
break anywhere in the sequence.

**And age confounds it, which this card takes seriously rather than glossing.**

| Group           | Created before 2026-07-01 |
| --------------- | ------------------------- |
| ≥700 words (13) | **12 of 13**              |
| <700 words (19) | **4 of 19**               |

Fifteen of the nineteen short entries were created in August and are five weeks
old. Their silence means nothing — `seo-audit.mjs` records that exact mistake
having been made once before. The matched-age comparison is 12 long against 4
short, and 0 of 4 is not evidence.

So the honest position is: **the length signal is real in the data and unproven
as a cause.** A different reading in this same audit firing — that high traffic
potential predicts _not_ surfacing — looked equally clean and reversed entirely
under the same age control.

## What is not confounded

Two things stand regardless of what causes the ranking.

1. **The library returns 3.9× its share of the corpus** (SA-16.1), measured
   across the full window. It is the best-performing layer on the site.
2. **It is written to two standards.** Thirteen entries average four paragraphs
   of argument; nineteen average one. That is a consistency problem in the
   layer that earns most, independent of search.

## Run

1. **Pick the three short entries that are old enough to have failed.** Only
   four of the nineteen predate July, and they are the only ones whose silence
   carries any information at all. Extend three of them — `goffman-frame-analysis`,
   `csikszentmihalyi-flow` and `overlie-standing-in-space` are the candidates —
   to the standard the long half already sets.
2. **Write them the way the long ones are written, not to a word count.** The
   thirteen that work say what the book argues, where it came from, and what
   this site takes from it. Padding three entries to 900 words would produce
   the number without the thing the number is a proxy for. This is why the card
   is `human`: it is writing about books, not a mechanical edit.
3. **Leave the other sixteen alone until the August cohort has aged.** They are
   five weeks old. Re-read them in 60 days — by then their silence will mean
   something, and if the long August entry is surfacing while the short ones
   are not, the hypothesis has independent support.
4. **Record the before and after.** Word count and query count for all 32, dated,
   so the comparison in 60 days is against something written down rather than
   re-derived.

## Verify

- Three pre-July short entries extended, each saying what its book argues and
  what the corpus takes from it.
- No entry was padded to hit a length; word count is an outcome, not a target.
- The other 16 are untouched.
- A dated before-table of word count and query count for all 32 exists.
- `npm run check` green, including the alias and citation guards.
- Re-read at 60 days against the rank tracker (SA-18.1) rather than against
  `gsc-pages`, and from 2026-02-01 (SA-16.1).

## Scoring

- **impact 3** — it acts on the layer measured at 3.9×, the source material
  exists because these books are already cited throughout the corpus, and the
  scope is three pages. Not 4: the causal claim is explicitly unproven, the
  matched-age cohort is 12 against 4, and a reading that looked this clean
  reversed under the same control earlier today. Not 2: the layer's return is
  measured rather than assumed, and the two-standards problem is real whatever
  search does with it.
- **radius 3** — three pages now and nineteen implicated, on 8% of the site that
  produces 33% of its impressions. Not 4: it changes no URL, no schema and no
  threshold, and step 3 explicitly holds sixteen of the nineteen back.
- **opportunity 9**
- **complexity 2** — three entries of roughly 400 additional words each, on books
  the corpus already cites. Not 3: step 3 keeps it to three pages rather than
  nineteen, which is what would have made it a content-at-scale card. Not 1:
  it is writing rather than editing, it needs the books, and step 2's rule
  against padding is the difference between doing this and gaming it.
- **roi 4.5** — mid-low, and right for a card whose central hypothesis is
  honestly unproven. If the 60-day re-read supports it, the remaining sixteen
  become a much stronger card than this one.
