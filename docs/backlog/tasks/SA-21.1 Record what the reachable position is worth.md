# SA-21.1 — Record what the position is worth

## What was found

Two SERPs read on 2026-09-25, both belonging to guides promoted from all 386
pages on the `PROMOTE_IF_FLOOR_UNDER` route.

### `theatre games` — floor DR 5

| Pos   | Result                          | DR    | Traffic |
| ----- | ------------------------------- | ----- | ------- |
| 2     | bbbpress "Drama Games for Kids" | 54    | 1,761   |
| 3     | Reddit r/Theatre                | 95    | 686     |
| 8     | Drama Notebook                  | 60    | 657     |
| 4     | dramaresource                   | 44    | 390     |
| 6     | Wenger                          | 54    | 316     |
| **7** | **burtsdrama**                  | **9** | **194** |
| 10    | YouTube                         | 99    | 185     |
| 9     | advancingartsleadership         | 27    | 136     |

Total visible traffic **4,325**; the top result takes **41%**.

### `del close` — floor DR 2, the best on the site

| Pos   | Result                  | DR     | Traffic   |
| ----- | ----------------------- | ------ | --------- |
| **1** | **Wikipedia**           | 97     | **1,014** |
| 3     | Second City             | 75     | 39        |
| 4     | IMDb                    | 94     | 8         |
| **8** | **homecomedytheater**   | **9**  | **7**     |
| 10    | Reddit                  | 95     | 2         |
| 6     | American Writers Museum | 65     | 1         |
| 7, 9  | YouTube, jimmycarrane   | 99, 37 | 0, 0      |

Total visible traffic **1,071**; **Wikipedia takes 95%** of it.

**The same evidence is worth 28× more on one than the other.** A DR 9 page at
position 7 on `theatre games` earns 194 visits a month. A DR 9 page at position
8 on `del close` earns 7. `top-guides.ts` cannot tell them apart, because the
only thing it records is that a low-DR page is present.

`del close` is the sharper case: SA-6.1 singles it out as the strongest
reachability evidence in the corpus, and it is a results page where a
single-entity encyclopedia answer absorbs almost everything.

## What this adds to what is already known

- **SA-6.1** asked whether the floor is _corroborated_ — is one low-DR result a
  fluke. Right question, and it still only concerns whether the position is
  attainable.
- **SA-20.1** asked who the results page is _for_. Also right, and qualitative.
- **This** asks what the attainable position _pays_. It is the one of the three
  that is a number already sitting in the same API response.

## Run

1. **Record the traffic at the lowest-DR position, not only its DR.** When
   `serp_top10_dr` is read, `serp-overview` returns traffic per result in the
   same call. One more field — what the most reachable result actually earns —
   costs nothing extra to collect and is the number the promotion decision
   needs.
2. **Record the top result's share too.** 41% against 95% is the difference
   between a page where positions are worth having and one where only position
   one is. It is a single division and it predicts the first number.
3. **Read the nine SERP-floor guides.** SA-6.1 already sends someone to read
   them and SA-20.1 adds a second reading to the same visit. Do all three at
   once: distribution, audience, and traffic at the reachable position. Reading
   nine results pages three times would be the waste this card is about.
4. **Do not change `PROMOTE_IF_FLOOR_UNDER` here.** The constant is defensible
   and the fix is to give it a second input, not to retune it blind. Once the
   nine readings exist, SA-4.1 owns the promotion block and can decide whether a
   floor with a seven-visit ceiling should promote anything.
5. **Expect `del close` to fail the new test and say so plainly.** It is a
   2,000-volume entity term whose answer is an encyclopedia entry. Keeping the
   page for readers while dropping it from sitewide promotion is a legitimate
   and probably correct outcome — the same shape as an `authority` verdict,
   arrived at by a different route.

## Verify

- All nine SERP-floor guides carry all three readings, taken in one pass.

- A written decision exists for `del close`.
- `npm run check` green.

## Scoring

- **impact 4** — this decides whether the site's largest internal-link lever
  buys anything. Two measured SERPs differ by 28× in what the promotion
  evidence is actually worth, and one of them is the page the repo holds up as
  its best case. The numbers are retrieved and come free with a call the repo
  already intends to make. Not 5: it moves no page and brings no visitor
  directly, and its value is in promotion not wasted.
- **radius 4** — a schema field inherited by 71 verdicts, the nine guides
  promoted on the floor route, and the comment block justifying the constant.
  Not 5: no content, no URLs, and step 4 keeps the promotion logic itself out of
  scope.
- **opportunity 16**
- **complexity 2** — two numbers from a response already being fetched, a schema
  field, and one decision. Not 1: step 5 anticipates demoting a page from
  sitewide promotion, which is a visible change on 386 pages, and the guards

  thing reverts in one commit.
- **roi 8.0** — level with SA-1.1 and SA-16.1. Pair it with SA-6.1 and SA-20.1:
  three cards, one visit to nine results pages, three fields recorded. Doing
  them separately triples the only expensive part.

**Three lost their floor entirely** — del-close, how-to-be-witty and
how-to-stop-caring-what-people-think — and left the promoted set as a result.
It went from **27 guides to 24**, driven by the data changing, not the rule.
`how-to-overcome-fear-of-failure`'s lone DR 1 result is gone from the top ten
altogether; its verdict is now `authority`.

**And the reachable position ranges from 7 visits a month to 1,938** — a factor
of 277 across guides the promotion block treated identically.

`schema.ts` gained `serp_floor_traffic`, `serp_top_share` and `serp_audience`
with the reasoning. `top-guides.ts` is unchanged, as step 4 required — the
promoted set moved because nine measurements moved.

Step 5 predicted del-close would fail the new test. It did, twice over: its
floor is DR 9 rather than 2, and the position is worth seven visits against
Wikipedia's 95%. It is no longer promoted and remains published for readers,
which is what the step said the right outcome would be.

Consequences carried, each re-dated with its account rather than lifted:
`guide-cohorts` (18 authority guides became 19), `anchor-diversity`
(`PROMOTED_TITLE_CEILING` 0.6 → 0.69 and `EXACT_RESIDUE` re-membered, both
because 24 promoted guides absorb more chrome anchors each than 27 did — no
guide gained a hand-written link). New guard: `serp-floor-evidence.test.ts`.
`npm run check` green, 271 files, 1411 tests.
