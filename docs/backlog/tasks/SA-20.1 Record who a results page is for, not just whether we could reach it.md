# SA-20.1 — Record who the results page is for

Two SERPs read on 2026-09-25 — the first anyone has read in twenty-two firings
of this audit.

### `improv games` — a different shape

Reddit holds position 2 with **three** r/improv and r/acting threads plus a
"more results from reddit.com" block; Quora takes two slots at position 5.
Roughly half the visible page is forum content. The rest is
improvencyclopedia.org (DR 49), hooplaimpro (DR 48), and two kids' sites.

## What this means

**The verdict is correct and insufficient.** `serp_min_dr: 5` on theatre-games is
accurate — a DR 9 blog holds position 7, so the page really is reachable from no
authority. That is why `top-guides.ts` promotes it from all 386 pages on the
SERP-floor route (see SA-6.1).

What no field records is that reaching it means competing with lesson plans for
children. The site would have to become a children's drama resource to win the
term it is currently spending its largest internal-link lever on.

That is the exact drift this epic's own target names — "without the corpus
drifting from the words it means" — being authorised by a measurement that
cannot see intent.

And `improv games` shows the gap has a second shape. Against a SERP that is half
Reddit and Quora, domain rating is not the binding constraint at all; the
question is whether a 3,000-word essay is the format the page rewards. No field
records that either.

## Run

1. **Add an audience or intent reading to the SERP fields.** Something short and
   factual recorded at the same time as `serp_min_dr`, because whoever reads the
   results page is already looking at it: who the top ten serves, and what format
   dominates. A free-text line is worth more than a taxonomy nobody maintains.
2. **Record it for theatre-games first, and decide what follows.** The options
   are real and the card does not pick one: keep the page and stop promoting it
   sitewide; keep the promotion and accept the term is aspirational; or retarget
   the page at a query the site's actual readers use. Write down which and why.
3. **Do the same for the promoted set's SERP-floor entries.** SA-6.1 lists nine
   guides promoted on a floor under DR 6. Those are the pages where reachability
   alone is doing the work, so they are where an unrecorded intent mismatch costs
   most. Six of them have no distribution recorded either.
4. **Do not add an intent field to `top-guides.ts`'s filters in this card.**
   Recording it is cheap and reversible; automating a promotion decision on a
   subjective reading is neither. SA-4.1 owns the promotion block and should get
   this as an input once the readings exist.
5. **Note the format observation separately.** "Half this SERP is Reddit" is a
   different fact from "this SERP is for teachers", and conflating them into one
   field will lose both.

## Verify

- The schema carries a field for who a results page serves, documented in
  CLAUDE.md's SEO section beside the existing SERP fields.
- `theatre-games` carries that reading and a written decision about its
  promotion.
- The nine SERP-floor guides from SA-6.1 each carry the reading.
- `top-guides.ts` is unchanged by this card.
- `npm run check` green.

## Scoring

- **impact 3** — this epic scores impact as _qualified_ traffic, and this is the
  field that would tell the difference. Two measured instances: a sitewide-
  promoted page pointed at a children's-education SERP, and a second term where
  the real competitor is Reddit rather than any domain. Not 4: it moves no page
  and brings no visitor, and its value is in effort not spent. Not 2: the
  evidence is retrieved and specific, and the gap it closes is the one the
  epic's own target names.
- **radius 4** — a schema field inherited by 71 recorded verdicts, the audit's
  buckets, and the reading behind the promotion block's SERP-floor route. Not 5:
  no content, no URLs, and step 4 deliberately keeps it out of the promotion
  logic for now.
- **opportunity 12**
- **complexity 2** — a field, a line of documentation, and a reading taken while
  already looking at a results page. Not 1: "who is this for" is a judgement
  that will be inherited by every future verdict, and step 5 exists because the
  obvious shortcut — one field for audience and format together — would make it
  useless. Not 3: nothing here needs a redirect or a rebuild of anything.
- **roi 6.0** — and worth pairing with SA-6.1, which sends someone to read the
  same nine results pages. Reading them once and recording both things is most
  of the cost of either card.
