## Three corrections this produced

- **The window was hiding two thirds of the evidence.** Every prior firing read
  2026-06-01 onward and saw 12 pages. From 2026-02-01 the same endpoint returns 34. Recorded against [[SA-14.1 Read the total, not the sample]], which found
  the complementary problem in the same tables.
- **Atoms are not "the layer that is working".** SA-5.1 said so on the narrow
  window and has been corrected: atoms are 45% of the site returning 20% of
  impressions, a lift of 0.4×. That strengthens SA-5.1's actual conclusion —
  an unmeasured layer that under-returns is a better reason to measure — but
  the framing was wrong.
- **CLAUDE.md was right all along.** "`reference` atoms are the library … and
  they punch above their weight in search" has been in the architecture section
  the whole time, unquantified and unacted on. It is 3.9×.

## Why this layer and not another

Three things line up here that line up nowhere else on the site.

1. **It already over-returns**, on first-party evidence, by the widest margin of
   any layer.
2. **Its queries are measurable.** SA-11.1 established that entity terms — people,
   books, institutions — come back populated from Ahrefs while the craft
   vocabulary returns null for `traffic_potential`, `difficulty` and
   `parent_topic` (SA-10.1). Book and author titles are entities. This is the
   one part of the corpus where the repo's existing machinery can size an
   opportunity.
3. **It is small and finite.** 32 pages, not 205.

And one thing that argues for urgency rather than patience: **every one of those
seven URLs changed on 2026-09-24**. SA-7.1 covers the migration; this card is
why it matters more than that card's ROI 4.5 suggests.

## Run

1. **Read `ref-viewpoints-bogart-landau` first.** Fifteen queries at an average
   position of 60 is a page Google has decided is relevant to a lot of things
   and ranks for none of them. Get its query list, check which of the 15 the
   page actually answers, and fix the snippet the way SA-1.1 does — this is the
   same defect with ten times the surface.
2. **Give the seven surfaced library pages measured metadata.** Entity terms
   return figures, so record `serp_checked`, `serp_min_dr` and `serp_verdict`
   per the schema. Where SA-5.1's placement question is unresolved, follow
   whatever it decided; do not invent a second pattern.
3. **Do not bulk-write the other 25.** The seven have evidence. The rest do not
   yet, and the honest move is to see whether the seven respond before touching
   a layer that is already the best-performing thing here.
4. **Report the layer table in the audit, dated.** `seo-audit.mjs` grades pages
   and has no notion of which layer earns. A five-line table of impressions per
   layer against pages per layer is the single most useful number this audit
   could print, and it is what would have caught the atom misreading two weeks
   earlier.
5. **Always read from 2026-02-01.** Fix the window in the audit's GSC block so
   the next reader does not repeat the mistake.
