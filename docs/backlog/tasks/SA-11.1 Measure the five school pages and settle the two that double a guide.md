# SA-11.1 — Measure the five school pages

## What was found

The five `/traditions` routes, measured in the build, against Ahrefs
`keywords-explorer-overview` retrieved 2026-09-25:

| Page                    | H1                                    | Inbound | Words | Term                     | Vol   | KD    | TP        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------- | ----- | ------------------------ | ----- | ----- | --------- |
| `/traditions/johnstone` | Keith Johnstone                       | **143** | 1,362 | keith johnstone          | 100   | 30    | 30        |
| `/traditions/ucb`       | Upright Citizens Brigade              | 100     | 1,353 | upright citizens brigade | 2,400 | 31    | 4,400     |
| `/traditions/annoyance` | Annoyance Theatre / TJ & Dave         | 87      | 1,041 | annoyance theatre        | 150   | **0** | 700       |
| `/traditions/close`     | iO and the Harold                     | 83      | 1,321 | io theater chicago       | 200   | **1** | **1,700** |
| `/traditions/spolin`    | Spolin and the Point of Concentration | 55      | 1,186 | viola spolin             | 700   | 42    | 300       |

None carries `target_keywords`, `serp_verdict` or `parent`, because none is a
content file — they are `page.tsx` routes, and the SEO schema only exists on
`BridgeFrontmatter`. No audit in the repo has ever counted them.

**Two of them are about the same subject as a guide.**

| Route page                                                                       | Guide                                                                                    | Verdict                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/traditions/spolin` — "Spolin and the Point of Concentration", 55 links, 1,186w | `/viola-spolin` — "Viola Spolin: The Woman Who Invented Theater Games", 82 links, 3,351w | **both plainly about Spolin**                                            |
| `/traditions/close` — "iO and the Harold", 83 links, 1,321w                      | `/del-close` — "Del Close: The Ideas That Still Run Improv", 123 links, 4,554w           | probably fine — one is the school, one is the man, and the titles say so |

The Spolin pair is the real one. Two pages, the same person, the same site,
137 internal links split between them, and no `parent` on either side because
one of them cannot have one. This is precisely the collision CLAUDE.md says to
check before creating an overlapping page, and it is structurally invisible to
the check.

**Why this is worth doing even though the numbers are small.** SA-10.1 found
that the vocabulary this site ranks for returns blank from Ahrefs for
`traffic_potential`, `difficulty` and `parent_topic` — seven of ten queries. The
entity terms are the exception: all six probed came back populated. So this is
the one corner of the corpus where the repo's existing machinery can actually
judge an opportunity, and `io theater chicago` at **difficulty 1 with 1,700
traffic potential** is the softest term twelve firings of this audit have
surfaced. The page exists, it is titled "iO and the Harold", and it has 83
inbound links.

## Run

1. **Read the five results pages.** `annoyance theatre` and `io theater chicago`
   first — difficulty 0 and 1 with a page already written is as close to a free
   position as this site gets. Expect `upright citizens brigade` and
   `viola spolin` to come back `authority`: a 2,400-volume institutional name at
   difficulty 31 and a biographical query at 42 are the shape of a SERP owned by
   Wikipedia and the institution itself. Recording that is a complete outcome.
2. **Decide where a route page's verdict lives.** These pages have no
   frontmatter. Either the traditions data moves into content files, or a small
   declared map sits beside the route. Do not invent a third pattern — SA-5.1
   asks the same question for atoms, and the two should be answered the same
   way. If SA-5.1 has landed, follow it; if not, keep this to a recorded reading
   and leave the placement to that card.
3. **Settle Spolin.** Two pages about one person is a decision to make, not a
   defect to panic about: differentiate them the way the Close pair already is —
   the school and its method versus the woman and her life — or canonicalise one
   to the other. Do **not** merge or redirect in this task. The guide is longer
   and better linked and carries the keyword metadata; the route page is part of
   a five-page set with a shared shape, and breaking that set to save 55 links is
   probably a bad trade.
4. **Leave the Close pair alone unless the SERP says otherwise.** "iO and the
   Harold" and "Del Close: The Ideas That Still Run Improv" already read as
   different pages about different things. If the results pages for
   `io theater chicago` and `del close` return the same competitors, revisit.

## Verify

- All five pages have a recorded `serp_checked`, `serp_min_dr` and
  `serp_verdict`, wherever step 2 decides those live.
- The declared volumes and difficulties are dated to their retrieval.
- The Spolin decision is written down, with which page owns the entity.
- No page was merged or redirected by this task.
- `npm run check` green, and `npm run seo:audit` either counts these five or
  says in one line why it does not.

## Scoring

- **impact 4** — `io theater chicago` at difficulty 1 with 1,700 traffic
  potential and `annoyance theatre` at difficulty 0 are the softest terms this
  audit has found in twelve firings, the pages already exist and are already
  linked, and someone searching either is unambiguously this site's reader. The
  figures are retrieved rather than assumed, which is what the epic's rule
  requires. Not 5: the volumes are 150 and 200 a month, no SERP has been read
  yet so `winnable` is a hypothesis, and two of the five will probably come back
  gated.
- **radius 2** — five route pages and two guides, seven of 386. It is a small
  corner. The honest note is that ROI ranks this near the bottom on radius while
  it may be the single most winnable item in the epic; the scale is doing its
  job, and an agent picking work should read the difficulty column as well as
  the queue order.
- **opportunity 8**
- **complexity 2** — five SERP readings, the same procedure SA-3.1 uses, plus a
  placement question that step 2 explicitly defers rather than solves. Not 3:
  steps 3 and 4 forbid the expensive moves, so nothing here needs a redirect or
  a reindex and all of it reverts in one commit. Not 1: judging `winnable`
  against `authority` on an institutional name is the judgement call this epic
