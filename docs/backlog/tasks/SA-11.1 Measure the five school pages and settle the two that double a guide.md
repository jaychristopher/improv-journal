# SA-11.1 — Measure the five school pages

## What was found

The five `/traditions` routes, measured in the build, against Ahrefs
`keywords-explorer-overview` retrieved 2026-09-25:

| Page                    | H1                                    | Inbound | Words | Term                     | Vol   | KD    | TP        |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------- | ----- | ------------------------ | ----- | ----- | --------- |
| `/traditions/johnstone` | Keith Johnstone                       | **143** | 1,362 | keith johnstone          | 100   | 30    | 30        |
| `/traditions/ucb`       | Upright Citizens Brigade              | 100     | 1,353 | upright citizens brigade | 2,400 | 31    | 4,400     |
| `/traditions/annoyance` | Annoyance Theatre / TJ & Dave         | 87      | 1,041 | annoyance theatre        | 150   | **0** | 700       |
| `/traditions/close`     | iO and the Harold                     | 83      | 1,321 | io theater chicago       | 200   | **1** | **1,700** |
| `/traditions/spolin`    | Spolin and the Point of Concentration | 55      | 1,186 | viola spolin             | 700   | 42    | 300       |

None carries `target_keywords`, `serp_verdict` or `parent`, because none is a
content file — they are `page.tsx` routes, and the SEO schema only exists on
`BridgeFrontmatter`. No audit in the repo has ever counted them.

**Two of them are about the same subject as a guide.**

| Route page                                                                       | Guide                                                                                    | Verdict                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/traditions/spolin` — "Spolin and the Point of Concentration", 55 links, 1,186w | `/viola-spolin` — "Viola Spolin: The Woman Who Invented Theater Games", 82 links, 3,351w | **both plainly about Spolin**                                            |
| `/traditions/close` — "iO and the Harold", 83 links, 1,321w                      | `/del-close` — "Del Close: The Ideas That Still Run Improv", 123 links, 4,554w           | probably fine — one is the school, one is the man, and the titles say so |

The Spolin pair is the real one. Two pages, the same person, the same site,
137 internal links split between them, and no `parent` on either side because
one of them cannot have one. This is precisely the collision CLAUDE.md says to
check before creating an overlapping page, and it is structurally invisible to
the check.

**Why this is worth doing even though the numbers are small.** SA-10.1 found
that the vocabulary this site ranks for returns blank from Ahrefs for
`traffic_potential`, `difficulty` and `parent_topic` — seven of ten queries. The
entity terms are the exception: all six probed came back populated. So this is
the one corner of the corpus where the repo's existing machinery can actually
judge an opportunity, and `io theater chicago` at **difficulty 1 with 1,700
traffic potential** is the softest term twelve firings of this audit have
surfaced. The page exists, it is titled "iO and the Harold", and it has 83
inbound links.

## Run

1. **Read the five results pages.** `annoyance theatre` and `io theater chicago`
   first — difficulty 0 and 1 with a page already written is as close to a free
   position as this site gets. Expect `upright citizens brigade` and
   `viola spolin` to come back `authority`: a 2,400-volume institutional name at
   difficulty 31 and a biographical query at 42 are the shape of a SERP owned by
   Wikipedia and the institution itself. Recording that is a complete outcome.
2. **Decide where a route page's verdict lives.** These pages have no
   frontmatter. Either the traditions data moves into content files, or a small
   declared map sits beside the route. Do not invent a third pattern — SA-5.1
   asks the same question for atoms, and the two should be answered the same
   way. If SA-5.1 has landed, follow it; if not, keep this to a recorded reading
   and leave the placement to that card.
3. **Settle Spolin.** Two pages about one person is a decision to make, not a
   defect to panic about: differentiate them the way the Close pair already is —
   the school and its method versus the woman and her life — or canonicalise one
   to the other. Do **not** merge or redirect in this task. The guide is longer
   and better linked and carries the keyword metadata; the route page is part of
   a five-page set with a shared shape, and breaking that set to save 55 links is
   probably a bad trade.
4. **Leave the Close pair alone unless the SERP says otherwise.** "iO and the
   Harold" and "Del Close: The Ideas That Still Run Improv" already read as
   different pages about different things. If the results pages for
   `io theater chicago` and `del close` return the same competitors, revisit.

pair left alone, nothing merged or redirected.

**Step 1 — the five results pages** (`serp-overview`, US, 2026-09-25; the

The card's expectation held for UCB and Spolin and was wrong, in the useful
direction, about the two soft ones. "io theater chicago" at difficulty 1
with 1,700 of potential and "annoyance theatre" at difficulty 0 are
navigational queries: people going to the theatre. The first result on each
is the theatre's own site with shows, classes and bar sitelinks, and the
potential is that homepage's traffic, which no page about the school can
have. Difficulty was low because nobody needs authority to be the thing
searched for. Both floors are DR 44 — chicagoplays.com's listing page at 1
and 2 visits — which is what a reachable position on a navigational page is
worth. Gated, all five, and that is the complete outcome the card allowed
for.

Two things worth keeping from the pages themselves. People Also Ask on
"annoyance theatre" asks what the Annoyance's style of improv is and who
founded it — the two questions the site's page answers — so the page's
chance on that term is the question box, not the ten blue links. And the
related figures read on the way: "io chicago" 500 / KD 2 / TP 1,800 and "io
improv" 60 / 1 / 1,900, both under the parent "io theater" (1,300);
"annoyance theater" 600 / 3 / 600; "ucb improv" 200 / 10 / 5,200 under
"upright citizens brigade". Same shape throughout — the potential is the
institution's.

**Step 2 — where a route page's reading lives.** SA-5.1 landed this morning
and its answer was: the same fields, on the layer's own record, only where
somebody read the page. Routes already had a record — `route-keywords.ts`,
"for pages that live on a route and have no frontmatter" — so it grew the
same way. `RouteKeyword` is now `BridgeTargetKeyword`, so a hub's keyword
carries difficulty, potential and parent where Ahrefs returned them, and
`ROUTE_SERP` holds a `SerpReading` per route plus `serp_query` and
`search_owner`, keyed by path. Its header no longer says these pages have
never been through a SERP check. The audit reads it through jiti, as
`route-pages.mjs` does, and prints one line: how many routes register a
keyword, how many have a reading, how many gated and owned. No third
pattern.

Keywords were registered only where the page's own title says the term, the
rule `keyword-collisions.test.ts` applies to hubs: johnstone, ucb (now with
"upright citizens brigade" beside "ucb improv") and annoyance (both
spellings). iO's page is "iO and the Harold" and its term is "io theater
chicago", so it registers nothing and keeps its reading alone — which is
right twice over, since the reading says the term is not the page's to
have. `HUB_TITLE_SOURCES` gained the two new routes and `hubTitles` reads
any tradition's label rather than ucb's only.

**Step 3 — Spolin, settled by ownership.** The guide `viola-spolin` — 3,351
words, 82 links, the keyword metadata, and an `authority` verdict read
today — owns the entity. `/traditions/spolin` is the school's page and part
of a five-page set; it registers no keyword, names the guide as
`search_owner`, and carries the guide's reading, which `route-serp.test.ts`
holds equal to the guide's so the two cannot drift. The differentiation the
Close pair already had — a `guide` link on the route page that hands the
biographical intent over — was already on the Spolin page too; the comment
in the route file now says so. Its own question, "What is Viola Spolin
technique?", is 20 a month and below the index's floor; "point of
concentration" and "spolin games" return nothing.

**Step 4 — the Close pair stays two pages.** "io theater chicago" returns
the theatre and its listings; "del close" returns Wikipedia's biography with
95% of the page (the guide's reading). No competitor in common, and the
titles already say which page is which.

**What moved.** No content, no URLs. `route-serp.test.ts` holds the five
readings on the shared fields, the H1 rule for what a tradition registers,
and the Spolin ownership. Registers 23 done, 18 open; the SA-11 story closes
with its only task. `npm run check` green, 281 files, 1,456 tests.
