# SA-17.1 — Settle who owns Viewpoints

## What was found

`content/bridges/viewpoints.md` declares six target keywords, `serp_checked:
2026-08-23`, `serp_min_dr: 6`, `serp_verdict: winnable`. It is promoted from all
386 pages.

Search Console, `gsc-keywords`, 2026-02-01 → 2026-09-25. Every Viewpoints query
the site has ever surfaced for, and the page Google chose:

**Fifteen queries, ~35 impressions, and `/viewpoints` takes none of them.** Four
of the six keywords the guide was written to win are being served by the book's
citation page.

The titles say why:

- guide — `Anne Bogart Viewpoints: The Nine Channels`
- citation — `The Viewpoints Book — Anne Bogart & Tina Landau (2005)`

Both lead with the same entity, and Google picked the citation.

**And the collision test could not have caught it.** CLAUDE.md names `parent` as
the test — "two pages competing is not detectable from distinct keyword strings,
it is detectable from a shared parent topic". The guide declares `parent:
"viewpoints"` on four keywords. The citation is an atom, so per SA-5.1 it has no
`parent`, no keywords and no verdict. There is nothing on the other side of the
comparison.

**It is not a one-off.** `ref-spolin-improvisation-for-theater` takes six
queries — "viola spolin improvisation for the theatre", "improvisation for the
theatre", and variants — while the `/viola-spolin` guide takes none. SA-11.1
found a third page about Spolin at `/traditions/spolin`.

## What this is not

It is not an argument that the citation should be demoted. Several of these
queries are genuinely about the book — "anne bogart viewpoints pdf", "the
viewpoints book" — and the citation is the right answer for those. The defect is
that one page is answering both intents and nothing decided that it should.

Nor is it a reason to merge or redirect. Both pages have a job.

## Run

1. **Split the fifteen queries by intent.** Book-intent — the pdf query, "the
   viewpoints", queries naming the authors — belongs to the citation.
   Concept-intent — "viewpoints acting", "viewpoints acting exercises",
   "viewpoints technique" — belongs to the guide. Write the split down; it is
   the decision the rest depends on.
2. **Retitle on that split, not on instinct.** The guide's title leads with
   "Anne Bogart Viewpoints", which is the citation's territory. If the guide is
   to own the craft queries its title should lead with the craft, and the
   citation's should lead with the book. Check the alias guard after, and watch
   position as the control the way SA-1.1 does — the citation currently sits at
   53–72 and a careless swap could cost both.
3. **Give the citation a `parent`.** This is the smallest durable fix: a
   `parent` on the reference atom is what would let the collision test run next
   time. It depends on SA-5.1's placement decision; if that has not landed,
   record the parent in the card and note the dependency rather than inventing
   a location.
4. **Check the whole library against the guides.** Spolin is confirmed. Do the
   same comparison for every reference atom whose subject also has a guide —
   `del-close`, `theatre-games`, `yes-and-improv`, `what-is-improv` — and report
   the count. That number is the size of this problem and nobody has it.
5. **Do not merge, redirect or unpublish anything.** Both pages earn their
   place, and SA-7.1 is still watching one URL migration land.

## Verify

- The fifteen queries are recorded with an owner each.
- Both pages' titles and descriptions reflect that split.
- The reference atom carries a `parent`, or the dependency on SA-5.1 is written
  down.
- The count of guide/citation subject overlaps across the library is reported.
- No page was merged, redirected or removed.
- `npm run check` green; re-read positions for both pages after 30 days, reading
  from 2026-02-01 per SA-16.1.

## Outcome

**Done 2026-09-25**, with step 3 recorded as a dependency rather than done.

**Step 1, the split.** Book-intent, the citation's: _anne bogart viewpoints_,
_viewpoints by anne bogart_, _viewpoints anne bogart_, _anne bogarts
viewpoints_, _anne bogart viewpoints pdf_, _the viewpoints_ — six queries, all
naming the authors or the book. Concept-intent, the guide's: _viewpoints
acting_, _viewpoints acting technique_, _viewpoints acting exercises_, _acting
viewpoints_, _viewpoints theatre_, _viewpoint theatre_, _viewpoints theater_,
_viewpoints drama_, _theatre viewpoints_ — nine queries, all naming the craft.

**Step 2, retitled on that split — and it turned into a retarget.** The guide
led with the citation's territory and now leads with the craft:
`Viewpoints Acting Technique: The Nine Channels` (46 chars), with a
description that opens on what the method _is_ and reaches Bogart and Landau
last. Body and H1 untouched, per SA-1.1's rule.

Two guards shaped it, and both were right. `bridge-serp-limits` requires a
guide's _primary_ keyword in its title, and the primary was `anne bogart
viewpoints` — book-intent by the split above. Putting the author back would
have restored the collision, so the craft keyword became primary instead:
`viewpoints acting` now leads `target_keywords`, the author-named term stays
declared and second, and the `search-names` twin fixture follows the new
primary with the reason written beside it. That is a retarget, and it is the
decision this card exists to make.

The citation had no authored description and shipped an extraction. It now
says what it is in its first two words — `The book. Bogart and Landau's
practical guide to Viewpoints and Composition — the standard written source
for the method.` — and the library page passes an authored description
straight through to the snippet. Two things were tried and reversed: an alias
`The Viewpoints Book` made the citation beat the concept atom for the bare
word "Viewpoints" in _site_ search, the reverse of the intent, so it came out;
and the `meta-descriptions` rule kept the publisher out of the description.

**Step 3 depends on SA-5.1, as the card allowed.** `parent` lives inside
`target_keywords` entries on `BridgeFrontmatter`; an atom has nowhere to carry
one. Giving the citation a parent so the collision test can see it is SA-5.1's
placement question, and inventing a third location here would be the thing
that card warns against. Recorded there; the reading is here.

**Step 4, the pattern.** Matching every reference atom's entity against guide
titles and keywords flags **9 pairs, 4 substantive**: Viewpoints (settled),
**Spolin** — `ref-spolin-improvisation-for-theater` against `viola-spolin` and
`improv-theory`, **Johnstone** — both citations against `improv-theory`, and
**Edmondson** — `ref-edmondson-psychological-safety` against
`psychological-safety`. Spolin is confirmed by GSC: the citation takes six
queries, the guide none. One pair per pass, watched thirty days; Spolin next.

**Step 4, the Spolin pair, read 2026-09-27.** Six queries surface, all
book-intent — _improvisation for the theatre_ and _improvisation for the
theater_, with and without the author's name — and all served by the
citation at 45 to 63; the guide's own term is gated (`authority`, the
estate's sites and Wikipedia) and surfaces for nothing. No collision to
settle. The citation shipped an extracted description, as the Viewpoints
one had; it now says what it is in its first two words, with the publisher
kept out. Next reading after a crawl: the same six queries, same page.

**Step 5 held.** Nothing merged, redirected or unpublished; three pages still
cover Viewpoints, and the `viewpoints` technique atom, which has never
surfaced, was left alone.
