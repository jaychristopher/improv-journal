# SA-9 — The subject is not a topic

The guides are filed into four clusters, and three of them are real: a pair of
guides inside Communication, Teams or Personal Growth shares three to four times
the entry atoms that two randomly chosen guides of the same size would. That is
a genuine neighbourhood, and the hubs built on it are doing honest work.

Improv Skills is not one. Its fifteen guides share 1.4 times chance, which is to
say barely more than any two guides picked at random from the whole corpus. It
is not a topic; it is a bucket labelled "about improv itself".

Tracker entry 290 found this on 2026-09-22 and nothing has been done with it.
What makes it worth a card rather than a note is where the cluster sits. It is
`CRAFT_CLUSTER` in `home-doors.ts` — the door the homepage offers to a visitor
who says they want to practise improv, which is the most qualified arrival the
site gets. It also holds most of the guides the site can actually win:
theatre-games, del-close, yes-and-improv, viewpoints, what-is-improv,
improv-prompts.

So the best-intent visitor is routed into the least structured part of the
corpus, and the pages that route is made of are the ones with a real chance of
ranking.
