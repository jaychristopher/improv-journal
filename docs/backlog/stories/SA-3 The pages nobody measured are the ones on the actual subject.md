# SA-3 — Measured adjacent, unmeasured core

The SEO discipline in CLAUDE.md is careful about absence: a missing
`serp_verdict` means nobody has looked, and that is recorded as the correct
state rather than guessed at. The discipline works. What nobody has asked is
*which* pages ended up in the unlooked-at pile.

They are not a random seven. They are the improv ones — the subject the site
actually teaches — and on today's numbers they are also the easiest terms it
owns. Meanwhile the measured, promoted set is largely party-question content at
several times the difficulty.

The consequence is structural, not editorial: a page with no verdict is not a
ranking candidate to any audit script, so the machinery built to find and
promote opportunity cannot see the pages closest to the site's own theme.
