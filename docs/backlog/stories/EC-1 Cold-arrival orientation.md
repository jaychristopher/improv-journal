# EC-1 — Cold-arrival orientation

103 of 205 concept pages render no context banner, because ContextBanner needs
both a thread and a path and half the atoms have neither. Every one of the 32
library pages is in that group, and library pages hold the best positions the
site has in Search Console — so the pages most likely to take a cold arrival
have the least orientation on them.
