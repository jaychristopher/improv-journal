# PG-1 — The school pools are thin for a hand of eight

The school room is the site's real edge (persona 1, the secondary drama
teacher with thirty pupils and fifty minutes), and the room's filter is the
guide's own: nothing that lands on a life, nothing that needs adult
knowledge, nothing with a built-in performer role. Applied to the bank on
2026-09-30 it leaves 47 relationship prompts of 94 and 47 audience questions
of 56. The cog now deals a hand of up to eight, so a teacher handing six
pairs a relationship each uses forty-eight in one lesson and the pool says
"that is all of them" in the second.

Forty more of each, written to the rubric and flagged honestly, roughly
doubles both. They go into the bank and the generated appendix, not the
guide's argued lists, and the title's count moves with them. The third task
turns the session's island-vocabulary scan into a guard, since the wording
rule is otherwise held only in a memory note.
