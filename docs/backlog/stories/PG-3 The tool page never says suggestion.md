# PG-3 — The tool page never says suggestion

The tool page owns "improv prompt generator" (150 a month, parent "improv
generator", read 2026-09-19) and says so in its title. The same search has a
sibling phrase in the field's own vocabulary — Andi Smith's page, Glasgow
Improv's, a Play Store app — and it is the same size: "improv suggestion
generator", 150 a month (US, 2026-09-30), parent not indexed. Its results
page has a DR 4 site at 10 earning 56 visits and a DR 20 site at 2 earning
108; all of them hand out nouns.

Nothing on the site says the phrase. One sentence of body prose that does,
and a reading recorded where routes keep theirs (`src/lib/route-keywords.ts`,
so the audit can see it), is the whole story. Not a heading, not the meta
description, not the rank tracker: `docs/seo/rank-tracker-keywords.txt`
forbids never-surfaced terms and the tracker charges per keyword. Verification
is Search Console in six to eight weeks, not the Ahrefs index.
