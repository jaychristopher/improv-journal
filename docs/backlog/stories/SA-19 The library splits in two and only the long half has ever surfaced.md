# SA-19 — Two libraries

SA-16 established that the library returns 3.9× its share of the corpus — 8% of
the site, 33% of its impressions. This is where that return comes from.

Sorted by body length, the 32 reference entries fall into two groups with a
211-word gap between them, the largest break in the sequence:

- **13 entries of 726 to 1,485 words** — the improv canon, mostly. Spolin,
  Bogart & Landau, Johnstone, TJ & Dave, Napier, the UCB manual.
- **19 entries of 337 to 515 words** — the adjacent citations. Goffman,
  Csikszentmihalyi, Sweller, Wickens, Meisner, Stanislavski, Brown.

All seven entries that have ever surfaced in Search Console are in the long
group. Not one of the nineteen short ones has ever appeared.

That looks decisive and is not, because the two groups are different ages: 12 of
the 13 long entries were created in April and 15 of the 19 short ones in August.
The matched-age cohort is 12 against 4, which supports nothing. `seo-audit.mjs`
already warns about exactly this — "their silence means nothing" — and the
warning caught a different reading wrong in this same audit firing.

What survives the confound is not a ranking claim but a structural one: the
library is written to two different standards, and the half written to the
shorter one is 19 pages on the best-performing layer this site has.
